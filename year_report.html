ANNUAL REPORTS

Each year, we provide comprehensive reports on the activities carried out during the previous year and share our plans and perspectives for future work. We transparently present our budget and the support provided to other organizations. We also invite you to explore a selection of photographs highlighting some of the most memorable moments of the year.
